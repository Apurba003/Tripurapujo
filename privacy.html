Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Tripura Pujo Guide is a static, client-side website. This policy explains what the site does when you use it.

Information stored on your device

Your selected pujos, visited status, added pujo entries, language and theme preference are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data is not sent to the site owner or a server by this website. Clearing browser storage removes it.

Location

If you choose the location feature, the browser may ask for your location. The coordinates are used in the current page to sort or build route links. The site does not store or transmit them itself. Your browser and device settings control permission.

Maps and external links

The map preview and route buttons use Google Maps. NTES, IRCTC, Instagram and other linked services have their own privacy policies and may receive information under their own terms when you open or load them. Google Maps may receive your request and approximate coordinates for the selected location.

Analytics, advertising and forms

This version has no Google Analytics, advertising pixels, account system, newsletter, payment page or contact form. No analytics identifier is embedded. If those services are added later, this policy must be updated before they are enabled.

Contact

No public email address is configured for this deployment. Do not send personal or sensitive information through third-party links.

Changes

Any future change to data collection or external services will be reflected here with a new update date.
